      3rd Week Creampuff

      Let's bake Creampuff

      Ingredients

        1. Unsalted Butter
        2. Vanilla Custard Filling
        3. Eggs
        4. Water
        5. Vanilla Extract
        6. All Purpose Flavour
        7. Sugar
        8. Cornflour
        9. Butter

      Steps

        1. Combine water, unsalted butter, and a pinch of salt in a medium saucepan over medium-high heat.
        2. Bring the mixture to a rolling boil until the butter is completely melted.
        3. Remove the pan from the heat and add all-purpose flour all at once, stirring vigorously with a wooden spoon or spatula until a smooth ball of dough forms
        4. Return the pan to low heat for 30 to 60 seconds while stirring constantly to cook out excess moisture.
        5. Remove from heat and let it cool slightly for a few minutes so the eggs do not scramble.
        6. Add eggs one at a time, mixing thoroughly after each addition until the dough becomes smooth, glossy, and falls off the spoon in a thick ribbon.
        7. Pipe or spoon mounds of dough about 2 inches apart onto the tray.
        8. Bake at 400°F (204°C) for 15 minutes, then lower the temperature to 350°F (170°C) and bake for another 30 minutes until golden brown and crisp.
        9. Make the cream filling in a chilled mixing bowl, whip heavy whipping cream, granulated sugar, and vanilla extract on medium-high speed until stiff peaks form.
        10. Insert the piping tip into the hole on the side of each cooled puff (or slice the tops off) and fill generously with whipped cream.
        11. Dust the tops with powdered sugar or drizzle with melted chocolate before serving.
